Ideas for the lesson
  • Discuss the 3D model: flat, round, thick, thin, smooth, symmetrical.
  • Draw a 2D shape first, predict the 3D result, then press Make 3D to check.
  • Draw half a vase as a full outline, then explain how the shape spins around its centre line.
  • Close the outline of every shape. Open lines turn into thin tubes.
  • Use Hole, then click inside a closed area (for example the middle of a donut) to cut a hole through the shape.
  • Keys: P pen, L line, O oval, B box, F fill, H hole, E eraser, Ctrl+Z undo.
